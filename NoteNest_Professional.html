Why NoteNest?

A better way to buy and sell on campus.

The concept is designed around a simple marketing problem: students may need used material, but discovery is scattered across informal channels.

🔎

Discoverability

One dedicated place makes available books easier to find than searching multiple chats.

🤝

Campus trust

A college-community approach keeps the marketplace relevant to the students it serves.

♻️

Second life

A book finished by one student can become an affordable resource for another.

Evidence to test before launch
  • How many students buy or sell used books each semester?
  • Which informal channels do they currently use?
  • Do students prefer a structured listing page over scattered WhatsApp messages?
  • Does the target audience actually discover campus information through community-style Instagram pages?

Important: these are assumptions to test, not established facts. A short campus survey or poll can validate them.
